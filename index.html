The Zaytoon Story

A Garden Born
from Passion

Where heritage meets the evening garden

Nestled in the heart of Multan — The City of Saints — Zaytoon Garden is a sanctuary of culinary artistry and timeless ambiance. Our open-air garden venue was conceived as a place where the soulful flavours of Punjab meet the romance of an illuminated evening sky.

Every dish that emerges from our kitchen tells a story — of spices hand-selected at Multan's oldest bazaars, of marinades perfected across generations, and of a commitment to authentic taste that has made us a landmark in the city's dining landscape.
